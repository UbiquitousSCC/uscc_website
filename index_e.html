 Welcome

Our laboratory is dedicated to the research and application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies, with a focus on implementing machine learning and deep learning techniques in real-world environments. In recent years, our lab has actively engaged in AI project development and partnered with industry on academia-industry collaboration programs. These initiatives include mobile application (App) development and data analysis platform design, emphasizing practical technology deployment and alignment with industry practices to enhance students' hands-on skills and competitive edge.
